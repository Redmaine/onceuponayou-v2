<p>Every parent knows the moment: the book comes out, and suddenly your little one is leaning in, wide-eyed, because the child on the page has <em>their</em> name, <em>their</em> hair, <em>their</em> favourite colour wellies. Personalised stories do something ordinary picture books can't — they put your child at the very centre of the adventure.</p>
<h2>It builds confidence</h2>
<p>When children see a version of themselves being brave, kind, or curious, they quietly absorb the idea that those things are possible for them too. A story where they rescue the lost puppy or sail across the starlit sea isn't just entertainment — it's a gentle rehearsal for real-world courage.</p>
<h2>It makes reading stick</h2>
<p>Attention is hard to win and easy to lose. But a child will sit still for a story that's about them. That extra minute of focus, night after night, is how a love of reading takes root.</p>
<h2>It turns bedtime into a ritual</h2>
<p>A book they ask for again and again becomes part of the fabric of the evening — a calm, predictable close to the day that helps little minds wind down and feel safe.</p>
<p>That's the whole idea behind Once Upon A You: a one-of-a-kind book, written and illustrated around your child, that they'll want to hear every single night.</p>
